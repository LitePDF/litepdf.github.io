Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Lite PDF ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Lite PDF does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect identifiers such as name, email, phone number, or location.

2. File Access

The App accesses PDF files only when selected by the user in order to display them. All file processing is performed locally on your device. We do not upload or transmit your documents to any server.

3. Local Data Storage

The App may store limited data locally on your device, such as:

  • Recently opened files
  • Favorites
  • Temporary cache files

This data remains on your device and is used only to improve usability.

4. Permissions

Lite PDF may request storage access to open files selected by you. Permissions are used only for core app functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No user data is shared with third parties.

6. Data Security

All data remains on the user’s device. We take reasonable measures to ensure the App operates securely, but no system can be completely risk-free.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
