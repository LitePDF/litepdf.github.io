Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

Lite PDF ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Process

Lite PDF does not require account registration and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other account information.

However, the App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used and to improve its functionality, stability, and user experience.

Firebase Analytics may automatically process information such as:

  • An app-instance identifier generated by Firebase;
  • App lifecycle information, such as app launches, sessions, and screen views;
  • Device and app information, such as the App version, device type, operating system version, language, and general technical information;
  • Coarse geographic information derived by Firebase from a masked IP address;
  • Interactions with App features, such as opening, saving, removing, searching, organizing, or sharing PDF files;
  • Reading-session events and approximate reading duration;
  • Settings and feature selections, such as theme, sorting, or screen-on preferences.

The App also generates a random identifier and provides it to Firebase Analytics as an analytics user ID. This identifier is not based on your name, email address, advertising ID, or account information and is not your real-world identity. It can associate usage events with the same App installation.

The current version of Lite PDF does not access or collect the Android Advertising ID and does not use analytics data for personalized advertising.

Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations also process an installation identifier, configuration requests, and technical information such as App and device details and connection IP addresses to deliver settings and manage feature availability. These requests can occur even when optional commercial features are disabled.

2. PDF Files and Document Content

Lite PDF accesses PDF files only when you select or open them, including when a file is sent to the App by another application.

PDF content is processed locally on your device. Lite PDF does not upload the contents of your PDF documents to our servers or to Firebase Analytics.

The App does not include PDF file names, file contents, document text, document passwords, search queries, group names, or document URIs in Firebase Analytics events.

For files received through temporary permissions, the App may create a local copy in its private application storage so that the document can remain available for later viewing. These private copies are not accessible to other applications unless you explicitly choose to share them.

3. Local Data Storage

Lite PDF may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Recently opened file records;
  • Favorites, custom group names, and file-to-group assignments;
  • Reading positions, page bookmarks, file references, and local document fingerprints used to restore access;
  • File names, file sizes, and last-opened timestamps;
  • App settings and preferences;
  • Private copies of documents for which permanent access could not be retained;
  • Temporary copies and generated PDFs for sharing, extraction, export, or printing;
  • Cached configuration, local feature-usage limits, and privacy choices where applicable;
  • A randomly generated analytics identifier.

This information is used to provide the App's functionality and improve usability.

You can clear recent-file history and temporary sharing cache through the App, and manage favorites, groups, and bookmarks. Eligible unused private copies can also be removed without deleting originals. PDF passwords entered for document operations are used for that operation and are not saved by Lite PDF. Uninstalling the App will normally remove data stored in its private application storage, subject to Android backup, restore, or device-transfer behavior controlled by your device or Google account. Private imported PDF copies are excluded from Android cloud backup, but may be included in direct device transfer. Settings and local records may be backed up or restored by Android. Exported files remain in the destination you select.

4. File Access and Permissions

Lite PDF uses the Android system file picker and URI permission system to access files selected by you.

The current version does not request broad access to all files stored on your device. The App may retain read access to a selected file when Android and the file provider support persistent URI permissions.

If persistent access is not available, the App may create a private local copy of the selected PDF as described above.

5. Exporting, Sharing, and Printing Files

Document export and page extraction run locally. Output is written to the destination you select; temporary output may remain in the App's cache. A selected destination may be a cloud storage provider with its own practices.

Sharing provides a PDF to the application you select through Android's sharing interface. Printing provides a document to the print service you select. Those applications or services may transmit or store the document under their own privacy policies.

Lite PDF does not upload your PDFs to Firebase or our servers for these operations and does not control a file after you provide it to another service.

6. Third-Party Services

Lite PDF uses Google Firebase Analytics, Remote Config, and Installations for the purposes described above. Google processes related information under its applicable terms and privacy documentation. Firebase data is encrypted in transit.

The App also includes Google Mobile Ads, User Messaging Platform (UMP), and Google Play Billing components for optional features described in Section 13. Including a component does not mean that its feature is active.

For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google Analytics Data Safeguards
  • Google Mobile Ads Data Disclosure
  • Google Play Terms of Service

7. How We Use Information

Analytics and configuration information is used for purposes such as:

  • Understanding how App features are used;
  • Measuring App sessions and feature interactions;
  • Identifying usability problems;
  • Improving performance, reliability, and functionality;
  • Delivering configuration settings and planning future improvements.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use the contents of your PDF documents for analytics, advertising, profiling, or marketing.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local information remains on your device until it is removed through the App, removed by Android, or deleted when the App is uninstalled, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Analytics information is retained according to our Firebase and Google Analytics retention settings. Configuration and installation information is retained according to the applicable Firebase service settings and practices.

If you would like to request deletion of analytics information associated with the App's randomly generated identifier, or if you have a privacy-related request, contact us at:

litepdf.feedback@outlook.com

Because the App does not require an account and the analytics identifier is not displayed to users, we may need additional information or technical cooperation to determine whether a particular analytics record can be located and deleted.

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use it to respond to your request and retain it as needed for support and applicable obligations. Please do not send document passwords or sensitive files unless necessary.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the App.

PDF documents and local records are stored within Android-controlled storage. Analytics information is transmitted using encrypted network connections. However, no storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Lite PDF is a general-purpose PDF utility that may be used by users of different age groups, including children where permitted by a parent, guardian, or applicable law.

The App does not require users to create an account and does not knowingly request names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact details, or the contents of PDF documents from children.

The App uses Firebase Analytics, which may process the limited technical and usage information described in this Privacy Policy, including an app-instance identifier, App interactions, device and App information, and coarse location derived from a masked IP address.

The current version does not collect the Android Advertising ID, does not display personalized advertising, and does not use PDF contents for analytics, advertising, profiling, or marketing.

Parents or legal guardians who believe that information relating to a child has been processed through the App may contact us to request review or deletion:

litepdf.feedback@outlook.com

11. International Processing

Information processed by Google services described in this Policy may be handled in countries or regions other than the one in which you live. Google applies its own data-transfer and security measures to information processed through Firebase and Google Analytics.

12. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing.

To submit a privacy request, contact:

litepdf.feedback@outlook.com

13. Optional Features and Changes in Processing

Advertising and in-app purchasing are disabled for this release. Lite PDF does not serve advertisements or offer purchases while these features are disabled. This is not a promise that all future releases or configurations will have the same features.

If advertising is enabled, Google Mobile Ads may process IP-derived approximate location, App interactions, diagnostic information, and device or App identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Android Advertising ID access is disabled in the current App configuration. UMP may process consent choices and technical information to manage privacy notices. Where required, consent is requested before relevant processing, and privacy choices are available through the App's settings.

If purchasing is enabled and you choose to buy, Google Play processes payment under its own terms. Lite PDF receives product identifiers, purchase tokens, status, and signed purchase records to verify and restore entitlements. It may retain a verified receipt locally; it does not receive your payment-card or bank-account details.

Before enabling processing that differs from the current release, we will update this Policy and the relevant store disclosures and provide any required notice or consent. PDF contents are not provided to these services for advertising or purchase verification.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Where required, additional notice or consent will be provided.
